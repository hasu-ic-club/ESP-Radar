# ESP Radar

**An open-source platform for bringing TI mmWave radar technology to the ESP32 platform.**

## Introduction

**ESP Radar** is an open-source hardware and software project that aims to make TI xWR series mmWave radar technology more accessible to ESP32 developers.

By leveraging the rich peripheral interfaces and powerful processing capabilities of ESP32 chips, including interfaces such as MIPI CSI, ESP Radar explores new possibilities for integrating mmWave radar sensors into embedded systems and IoT applications.

The project provides hardware designs, software implementations, technical documentation, and practical development guides to help developers build their own radar-based applications. Whether you are working on academic research, smart home systems, security applications, human presence detection, or other innovative projects, ESP Radar aims to provide the building blocks and knowledge needed to get started.

## What We Provide

ESP Radar is designed to be more than just a collection of drivers. It aims to provide a practical and reusable development foundation covering both hardware and software.

- **Hardware Designs:** Reference hardware designs and implementation guidelines for integrating ESP32 platforms with TI mmWave radar devices.
- **Software and Firmware:** Open-source implementations for radar initialization, configuration, data acquisition, and peripheral integration.
- **Technical Documentation:** Development notes, hardware considerations, interface specifications, and step-by-step guides based on practical implementation experience.
- **Data Acquisition and Processing:** Tools and examples for capturing raw radar data and making it available for further analysis and signal processing.
- **Extensible Architecture:** Modular implementations that developers can adapt, extend, and incorporate into their own projects.

## Applications

The possibilities of mmWave radar extend across many fields, including:

- **Research and Development:** Radar signal processing, sensing algorithms, human activity recognition, and experimental radar systems.
- **Smart Home and IoT:** Presence detection, motion sensing, occupancy monitoring, and intelligent automation.
- **Security and Surveillance:** Perimeter monitoring, intrusion detection, and motion-based sensing.
- **Custom Embedded Systems:** Building specialized radar-enabled devices and exploring applications beyond conventional sensing solutions.

These are just a few examples. We look forward to seeing what the community builds with ESP Radar.

## Open Source, Open Collaboration

ESP Radar is developed with an open-source philosophy. We aim to make our hardware designs, source code, documentation, and development experience openly available under permissive open-source licenses, allowing developers to learn from, reuse, modify, and build upon the project.

You are welcome to use ESP Radar as a reference for your own designs, adapt its components to suit your requirements, or develop entirely new applications based on the technologies and ideas presented here. Please refer to the individual project directories for their respective license terms.

**Community contributions are always welcome!**

Whether you are interested in improving the firmware, refining hardware designs, adding support for new radar devices, developing signal processing algorithms, improving documentation, or sharing your own applications, your contributions can help make ESP Radar better.

Feel free to open an issue, submit a pull request, share your ideas, or simply showcase what you have built.
